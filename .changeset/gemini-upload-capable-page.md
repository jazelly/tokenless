Require Gemini file-upload requests to use a conversation page that exposes both the composer and an enabled Upload & tools control. If the selected conversation lacks that control, open a fresh Gemini conversation before running `workspace.ensure` or selecting files.

要求 Gemini 文件上传请求使用同时显示输入框和已启用 Upload & tools 控件的对话页面。如果选中的对话缺少该控件，则在运行 `workspace.ensure` 或选择文件前打开新的 Gemini 对话。
